# Midnight Falls — Boss Guide
**March on Quel'Danas | Season 1 | Boss 2 of 2 | Final Boss**

_Guide for RaidLens analysis context. Covers all difficulties; Mythic differences called out explicitly._

---

## Overview

Midnight Falls is fought against **L'ura**, an elven void ranger, in a three-phase fight with an intermission separating Phase 1 from Phase 2. The fight is one of the most mechanically diverse encounters in Season 1: Phase 1 involves a memory game, bouncing glaives, and Crystal management; the Intermission requires healing through stacking absorbs; Phase 2 takes the raid inside the Darkwell to destroy Void Cores with targeted beams; Phase 3 is a pure DPS race with Crystal-based mechanics as the survival tool.

The central persistent resource is the **Dawn Crystal** — friendly Crystals that, once healed to full and picked up, provide light that protects players from the Midnight darkness in Phase 3. Every decision in Phase 1 is partly motivated by securing enough Dawn Crystals for Phase 3 survival.

The **Darkwell** at the room's center is an instant-kill zone at all times in Phase 1 and 3. The Dark Quasar also periodically fires deadly spinning beams from it throughout Phase 1.

---

## Phase 1: The Memory and the Crystals

### Boss Abilities

**Dark Quasar** — The Darkwell at the room's center is a permanent death zone. Periodically, beams shoot outward from the Darkwell and rotate around the room, dealing massive damage to any player hit. Dodge the rotating beams while handling other mechanics.

**Death's Dirge (Memory Game)** — L'ura flashes a sequence of Runes in a pattern around herself. After the flash, those same Rune icons appear over marked players' heads. L'ura then shoots a laser from her position that rotates clockwise, destroying the Runes from players' heads in the order the laser touches them. The raid must form a semicircle of marked players in the **same left-to-right order as the original flash sequence** — players who are destroyed in the wrong order take massive damage. Read the sequence, communicate positions, and hold position until your Rune is destroyed.

**Safeguard Matrix** — Three Safeguard Matrix adds spawn around L'ura and rotate around her. Each add reduces L'ura's damage taken by 33% per living add. Each add has a buff called **Safeguard** that keeps it alive. Interrupt the add's cast to remove a stack of Safeguard; when all Safeguard stacks are removed from an add, it is destroyed. Interrupt all three in rotation — they must all die.

**Disintegration** — L'ura fires a burst of raid-wide damage and simultaneously spawns two types of Crystal mobs:
- **Midnight Crystals (enemies):** Cast **Cosmic Fracture**, which wipes the raid if it completes. Kill these before the cast finishes.
- **Dusk Crystals (friendly):** Heal these to full health to transform them into **Dawn Crystals**. Players holding Dawn Crystals are critical for Phase 3.

**Dawn Crystal** — A fully-healed Dusk Crystal becomes a Dawn Crystal that floats near the ground. Players pick them up using their extra action button and can drop them at will. Holding a Dawn Crystal applies a persistent DoT to the carrier. If a Dawn Crystal is left on the ground for more than 3 seconds, it begins pulsing massive raid-wide damage until picked up again. Manage handoffs carefully — never leave a Crystal unattended.

**Heaven's Glaives** — L'ura fires several glaives that bounce around the entire room until Phase 1 ends. Dodge them throughout all Phase 1 mechanics. They add consistent movement pressure to everything else.

**Heaven's Lance** — Tankbuster. Five consecutive hits, each applying a stack of **Impaled** that increases Lance damage taken by 50% for 25 seconds. Tank swap after each full five-hit combo — the stacks are too dangerous for the same tank to absorb twice in a row.

**Shattered Sky** — Unavoidable raid-wide rot damage throughout Phase 1.

**Tears of L'ura** (Heroic+) — When a Dawn Crystal carrier is hit by a Cosmic damage ability (such as the beam mechanics), several soak circles spawn near them. A player must intercept each soak circle or the raid takes massive damage per missed soak.

## Intermission: Total Eclipse

The Darkwell expands during Total Eclipse — beams still project from it but stop rotating (they remain static for the duration). Players are continuously marked throughout the Intermission, shooting beams from their own bodies. All players constantly receive a small **healing absorb shield** — it stacks every second, requiring sustained healer output throughout. This is the primary healer cooldown window.

---

## Phase 2: Inside the Darkwell

L'ura pulls the raid into the Darkwell dimension. The boundary of the room is enforced by **Iris of Oblivion** — players who move outside the bounds are instantly killed.

**Galvanize** — L'ura marks players who must aim a targeted line mechanic to strike and destroy **Void Cores** spinning around the room's perimeter. Aim accurately — each Core must be hit.

- **Heroic:** Galvanize becomes a high-damage group soak mechanic. Assign soaking groups ahead of time. Cosmic damage note: Crystal carriers must drop their Crystals before soaking and pick them back up immediately after — being hit by Galvanize damages the Crystal.

**Cosmic Fission** — When a Void Core is destroyed, it briefly pulls nearby players toward it. Shortly after, L'ura absorbs the destroyed Core, rapidly pulling it inward alongside a burst of raid-wide damage and dealing massive damage to any player in the Core's path during absorption. Move to safe positions between Cores.

**Abyssal Pool** — Unavoidable rot damage throughout Phase 2.

The phase ends when all Void Cores are destroyed and absorbed.

---

## Phase 3: The Final Darkness

The raid returns to the main platform. L'ura must die before she casts **Devouring Cosmos** three times — each cast destroys the platform. This is the enrage condition.

**Torchbearer** — Players holding Dawn Crystals emit light that protects all nearby allies from the Midnight darkness — players outside this light take constant massive damage. Crystal carriers are the lifeline of the entire raid in Phase 3. Protect them. Stay near them.

**Dark Archangel** — L'ura fires a deadly blast and leaves a **zone of darkness** behind it. A player holding a Dawn Crystal must use their extra action button to activate a **protective shield** for the raid during the blast. Immediately after, all players must exit the darkness zone — it deals damage to anyone inside.

**Dark Constellation** — Star orbs crash into the platform, dealing massive damage to anyone close when they land. After landing, the stars connect with each other via light beams. Any player standing inside the connecting beams takes damage. Move to the open spaces between stars.

**Light Siphon** — Soak circles appear on the platform. Players must stand inside them to drain them — if any circle is not fully drained before it expires, it explodes and wipes the raid.

## Heroic Changes

- **Tears of L'ura:** When a Dawn Crystal carrier takes Cosmic damage, soak circles spawn. A player must catch each one.
- **Galvanize (Phase 2):** Becomes a group soak mechanic requiring pre-assigned groups.
- **Light Siphon (Phase 3):** Introduced on Heroic.

## Individual Mistakes to Flag (3+ Pulls Threshold)

- **Dark Quasar beam hits** — Rotating beams are telegraphed and predictable. Repeated hits = not tracking beam rotation.
- **Heaven's Glaives hits** — Bouncing glaives are consistently present throughout Phase 1. Repeated hits = standing still or not tracking glaive trajectories.
- **Dark Constellation beam hits (Phase 3)** — Connecting beams between stars are stationary after landing. Repeated hits = not moving to safe positions.
- **Dark Archangel darkness zone** — Player repeatedly taking damage inside the zone after the shield = not exiting the darkness.
- **Death's Dirge incorrect position** — Player repeatedly taking the large damage hit = standing in the wrong order position during the rune sequence.

## Quick Tips

- **Phase 1 Crystal priority:** Assign dedicated healers to the Dusk Crystals during Disintegration while DPS kill the Midnight Crystals. Missing a Dusk Crystal heal means one fewer Dawn Crystal carrier in Phase 3 — directly reducing survivability.
- Memorize the Death's Dirge sequence as it flashes; do not wait for the laser to start before moving. Call positions immediately after the flash.
- In Phase 2, Crystal carriers must drop their Crystals and be clear before soaking Galvanize — the 3-second floor timer is very tight, so have a non-carrier ready to immediately pick up any dropped Crystal.
- Phase 3 is a pure race — use Bloodlust here. Assign Crystal carriers to each healer subgroup so no player is ever out of light coverage.
- Light Siphon circles can overlap — coordinate drain assignments so every circle has enough players and no player is pulled in two directions at once.
