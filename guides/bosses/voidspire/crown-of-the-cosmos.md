# Crown of the Cosmos — Boss Guide
**The Voidspire | Season 1 | Boss 6 of 6**

_Guide for RaidLens analysis context. Covers all difficulties; Mythic differences called out explicitly._

---

## Overview

Crown of the Cosmos is fought against Alleria (Ranger Captain) alongside three sub-bosses: Demiar, Vorelus, and Morium (the Undying Sentinels). The fight has three main phases separated by two intermissions, with the platform progressively fracturing into three separate chunks by Phase 3.

The central mechanic across all phases is **Silverstrike Arrows** — targeted player-aimed lines that pierce through enemies and remove their void immunity buffs. The entire fight revolves around aiming these arrows correctly.

---

## Phase 1: The Undying Sentinels

Three Undying Sentinel sub-bosses are alive at the start: Demiar, Vorelus, and Morium. They each pulse raid-wide damage and have their own abilities. None can be killed until their death immunity is removed by a **Silverstrike Arrow**.

**Silverstrike Arrow** — A few players are targeted by Silverstrike Arrows. The arrow shoots in a straight line from the boss through the targeted player and continues until it hits a wall, passing through any enemies or players in its path and removing void immunity from them. Targeted players must **reposition themselves** so the straight-line arrow passes through the Sentinel(s) that need their immunity stripped. Being hit by the arrow deals damage to the player.

- **Heroic:** Being hit by a Silverstrike Arrow applies a debuff that increases arrow damage taken by 800% for 8 seconds. Alternate between players who soak arrows.

**Grasp of Emptiness** — A player is marked with a circle on the ground. Three obelisks appear nearby, each connected to the marked player by a beam that moves as the player moves. The player must position themselves so all three beams shoot away from the raid and through open space. Other players must move out of the initial circle and away from all beam paths — both the circle and beams deal damage.

- **Heroic:** Two players are simultaneously marked.

**Void Expulsion** — An orb spawns near a ranged player, explodes after a few seconds, and leaves a permanent puddle. Deals raid-wide damage on explosion. Ranged players should cluster near existing puddles to consolidate puddle drops.

**Interrupting Tremor (Demiar)** — A pulsing AoE within 40 yards that damages and silences players. Move out of Demiar's range to avoid the silence.

**Ravenous Abyss (Vorelus)** — A pulsing 15-yard AoE around Vorelus that deals damage and reduces player damage output by 70% to anyone hit. Move out of the radius.

**Corrupting Essence** — Void Droplets spawn periodically. When they die, they splash a puddle on the ground — anything hit by the splash takes 30% increased damage for 30 seconds, including enemy bosses. Kill Droplets directly on top of a Sentinel to splash the damage buff onto them, dramatically speeding up kills.

**Null Corona** — A massive healing absorb shield is applied to a random player. Must be healed off. If dispelled, the remaining absorb jumps to another player — avoid dispelling unless the player is in danger. Heal through it.

**Dark Hand (Morium)** — Morium's tankbuster. Heavy physical and magic damage with a knockback. Use a personal defensive.

---

## Intermission 1

When all Sentinels die, everyone is knocked into the air and Alleria crashes into the center of the arena, deepening the three cracks in the platform.

**Stellar Emission** — Players are slowly dragged toward the cracking center. The pull intensifies over time. Players also take stacking raid-wide ticking damage every 2 seconds — eating a Silverstrike Arrow during this intermission removes your stacks. Several circles mark where orbs will explode, launching players upward.

**Silverstrike Barrage** — Multiple waves of Silverstrike arrows shoot from Alleria. Dodge them (or absorb intentionally to clear Stellar Emission stacks, but the 800% debuff on Heroic makes this risky after the first hit).

---

## Phase 2: The Simulacrum

Alleria summons a void clone (her Simulacrum). Alleria and the Simulacrum share health. If they get within 30 yards of each other, both gain a stacking 10% damage buff. Keep them apart. The phase ends when the Simulacrum dies.

**Void Expulsion (P2)** — Orbs continue spawning; this is now area denial as well as a soft enrage — the room fills with puddles. Kill the Simulacrum before the room is unnavigable.

**Silverstrike Ricochet** — Several players are marked with Ranger Captain's Mark. Instead of a direct arrow, a single arrow bounces between all marked players. Players must coordinate positioning so the bouncing arrow passes through **Venombite Crawler** adds that have death immunity. Aim together to break add immunity in sequence. If a Crawler reaches 100 energy without dying, it gains 300% movement speed, 500% damage, and CC immunity.

**Cosmic Barrier** — A 15% health shield appears on the Simulacrum. While it persists, the raid takes pulsing damage. Break the shield immediately.

**Voidstalker Sting** — A stackable 10-second DoT on several players. On Heroic, this extends to 25 seconds. Standing in the path of a Silverstrike Arrow removes this DoT.

**Null Corona** — Same as Phase 1. Heal through the absorb.

**Rift Slash** — The Simulacrum's tankbuster. Deals physical damage and applies a 10% stat reduction debuff for 20 seconds (stacking). Swap at around 3 stacks.

**Volatile Fissure** — The room's three cracks now apply a DoT to any player who crosses them. Stay on your side of the fissures.

---

## Intermission 2

Same pattern as Intermission 1 when the Simulacrum dies. After this second intermission, the platform fractures completely into three separate chunks.

---

## Phase 3: Broken Platform

The fight continues on three separated platform sections. Players must jump between sections using **Devouring Cosmos** feathers. Phase 3 ends when Alleria is killed — she must die before she casts Devouring Cosmos three times, otherwise the entire platform is consumed.

**Grasp of Emptiness (P3)** — Same as Phase 1, but now played on a smaller, broken platform. Positioning space is more constrained.

**Aspect of the End** — Several players are tethered by arrows. While tethered, players take 10% reduced healing (stacking). Break the tether by running outside the circle range. Breaking a tether deals burst raid-wide damage and applies a 300% physical damage taken vulnerability debuff. One tether is always on the active tank — this is the tank swap mechanic for Phase 3. Break tethers one at a time.

**Devouring Cosmos** — Alleria calls void energy to destroy the current platform section. Feathers scatter from her quiver — each player must pick up a feather and use the buff to jump to a new platform section before the void energy kills them. Any player who touches the void energy takes massive damage.

**Null Corona** — Same as previous phases. Heal through it.

**Voidstalker Sting** — Same stackable DoT. Heal through it in Phase 3 (no arrows to cleanse in this phase).

---

## Heroic Changes

- **Silverstrike Arrow:** Arrow debuff (800% increased damage) after being hit.
- **Void Expulsion:** More frequent and more targets — especially in Phase 3.
- **Grasp of Emptiness:** Two players simultaneously marked.
- **Intermission 2:** Rotating walls of orbs force players to briefly cross platform cracks.
- **Silverstrike Barrage:** Same debuff as single arrows.

## Individual Mistakes to Flag (3+ Pulls Threshold)

- **Grasp of Emptiness beam hits** — Player repeatedly caught in a moving beam = not tracking the marked player's position and moving clear.
- **Ravenous Abyss** — Player repeatedly inside Vorelus's AoE = not maintaining distance from that Sentinel.
- **Interrupting Tremor silence** — Player repeatedly silenced = standing too close to Demiar.
- **Void Expulsion puddles** — Standing in already-placed puddles = not watching the floor.
- **Silverstrike Barrage** — Repeated hits during intermission = not tracking beam directions.

## Quick Tips

- In Phase 1, position marked Grasp of Emptiness players on the opposite side of the room from the Sentinels being damaged — their beams shoot away from the fight.
- Kill Void Droplets directly on top of priority Sentinels to splash the 30% damage bonus for big kill windows.
- Assign specific Silverstrike Ricochet players in Phase 2 ahead of time and pre-position them so the bounce path is clean.
- In Phase 3, pick up your feather and start moving toward the jump point before Devouring Cosmos finishes its animation — the window is shorter than it looks.
- Never cross platform fissures in Phase 2 — the Volatile Fissure DoT stacks and can be lethal during other damage events.
