## Overview

Marksmanship Hunter is a ranged physical DPS specialization built around slow-casting but hard-hitting, cooldown-limited abilities — primarily **Aimed Shot** and **Rapid Fire** — supported by instant filler shots. The core gameplay loop revolves around two proc/stack mechanics: **Precise Shots** (generated by Aimed Shot and Rapid Fire; must be spent on Arcane Shot or Multi-Shot before generating another) and **Spotter's Mark** (which buffs Aimed Shot). Patch 12.0.5 brought a moderate rework that reintroduced **Explosive Shot** along with talents **Precision Detonation** (buffs Aimed Shot shortly after use) and **Shrapnel Shot** (grants Lock and Load).

Damage profile: strong, front-loaded burst damage on single targets aligned to the 2-minute **Trueshot** window, with reasonable flexibility from Aimed Shot's two charges. The spec is a pure DPS with no healing throughput contribution.

Survivability profile: weak passive defenses offset by several strong active defensive cooldowns. The spec must use those cooldowns deliberately to survive lethal windows.

Primary weakness: a meaningful portion of the rotation (Icy Veins estimates roughly 40%) is spent stationary casting Aimed Shot and channeling Rapid Fire, so heavy-movement encounters cost damage. The spec also struggles with 2-target cleave situations.

## Role in Raid

- **Role:** Ranged DPS. Not a healer, not a tank.
- **Raid buff/debuff it brings:** **Hunter's Mark** — a single-target damage-taken increase that makes the marked enemy take more damage from all sources (Icy Veins describes it as effectively a universal ~3% single-target damage increase, which is the primary reason the spec holds a raid spot). It does NOT bring a unique party-wide stat buff like an aura.
- **Bloodlust / Heroism equivalent:** No. Hunters do NOT provide a Bloodlust-equivalent effect. (Their pet does not provide it in Midnight — Primal Rage is a Beast Mastery pet ability, not a Marksmanship raid tool. Treat MM as having no Lust.)
- **Battle res:** No. Hunters do not have a combat resurrection.
- **Group defensive / external:** **Roar of Sacrifice** (spell ID 53480) — reduces a friendly target's damage taken by 15% for 10 seconds, 2-minute cooldown. This is a usable raid external on another player. **Misdirection** (spell ID 34477) transfers the Hunter's threat to a target for a short window (threat tool, not a defensive).
- **Mobility profile:** Above average for a caster-style ranged spec. **Disengage** (backward leap), **Aspect of the Cheetah** (spell ID 186257, +90% run speed burst, 3-minute cooldown), and the ability to fire instant shots while moving. Aimed Shot and Rapid Fire are stationary, so sustained movement still reduces output.

## Abilities Reference

Spell IDs below were each confirmed on the live Wowhead spell page listed in Sources. Where a numeric value conflicted between a tooltip page and a written guide, the conflict is flagged in Known Gaps.

Core damage:
- **Aimed Shot** (spell ID 19434) — heavy Physical damage, ~2.5s cast, costs 35 Focus, 40 yd range. Two charges. Generates Precise Shots.
- **Rapid Fire** (spell ID 257044) — channeled burst of shots, 40 yd range. Generates Focus and Precise Shots. (Cooldown: see Known Gaps — live spell page showed 16s; some guides list 20s.)
- **Trueshot** (spell ID 288613) — primary 2-minute burst cooldown, 15s duration. Grants ~10% crit, increases crit damage, speeds charge cooldown recovery, and increases Spotter's Mark generation.
- **Kill Shot** (spell ID 53351) — execute, instant, 10 Focus, 40 yd range. Usage-gated (low-HP targets / procs).
- **Arcane Shot** — instant Arcane damage filler; primary Precise Shots spender on single target.
- **Multi-Shot** — instant AoE filler; primary Precise Shots spender in AoE; activates Trick Shots.
- **Steady Shot** — Focus-generating filler.
- **Explosive Shot** — reintroduced in 12.0.5; filler / talent payoff (Precision Detonation, Shrapnel Shot → Lock and Load).
- **Volley** — ground AoE; also used to activate Double Tap in current builds.
- **Black Arrow** — cooldown-gated hard hitter used in opener and priority.
- **Blighted Arrow** — talent/proc shot that appears in SimC damage output; appears to be a dark-themed projectile in the Black Arrow talent tree.
- **Bleak Arrows** — talent/proc ability appearing in SimC damage output alongside Black Arrow; likely a periodic or follow-up effect from the same talent cluster.
- **Master Marksman** — passive talent that deals bonus Physical damage; appears as a significant proc contributor (~7.9% of total damage in SimC).

Key passives / procs:
- **Precise Shots** — proc from Aimed Shot / Rapid Fire; buffs Arcane Shot and Multi-Shot. Must be spent before regenerating.
- **Spotter's Mark** — stacking buff that increases Aimed Shot damage.
- **Trick Shots** — AoE-enabling buff (Multi-Shot / Aimed Shot cleave).
- **Lock and Load** — free/instant Aimed Shot proc (via Shrapnel Shot talent).

## Rotation / Priority

This is a **logical priority order**, not a DPS benchmark. Exact sequencing depends on talent build, Focus, charges, and active procs; treat the per-pull log as ground truth, not this list.

Single-target opener (Icy Veins, 12.0.5):
1. Pre-cast Aimed Shot before pull
2. Black Arrow
3. Aimed Shot
4. Volley (to activate Double Tap)
5. Rapid Fire
6. Trueshot (line up with potion/racials)
7. Black Arrow → Rapid Fire → Black Arrow → Aimed Shot, then into priority

Single-target sustained priority (condensed):
1. Maintain Trueshot windows (Trueshot, ideally right before an Aimed Shot)
2. Rapid Fire on cooldown
3. Aimed Shot before capping 2 charges
4. Spend Precise Shots with Arcane Shot (or Kill Shot when available) after Aimed Shot — never overwrite an unspent Precise Shots proc
5. Aimed Shot on cooldown
6. Explosive Shot / Steady Shot as filler

AoE priority (condensed):
1. Keep Trick Shots active for Aimed Shot / Multi-Shot cleave
2. Spend Precise Shots with Multi-Shot (then Arcane Shot)
3. Rapid Fire to maintain the buff supporting Aimed Shots
4. Volley, Explosive Shot, Aimed Shot on cooldown
5. Steady Shot / Explosive Shot as filler

Note: every Arcane Shot or Multi-Shot should ideally be cast with Precise Shots active. Repeatedly casting filler without Precise Shots, or letting Aimed Shot charges cap, are the most common rotational losses.

## Defensives

Each entry includes a RaidLens usage note: how to judge from a log whether the defensive was used well during a damaging window.

- **Exhilaration** (spell ID 109304) — heals the Hunter for 30% of max HP (and the pet). Cooldown 2 minutes per the live spell page. (See Known Gaps: a written guide listed 1 minute; the live tooltip showed 2 minutes.)
  - *RaidLens usage:* A pure self-heal, not a damage-reduction wall. Expect it used to recover after a big hit or a DoT/soak, not necessarily pre-cast. If the player took repeated avoidable damage and Exhilaration sat unused while they died, that is a flag.
- **Survival of the Fittest** (spell ID 264735) — reduces all damage taken by 30% for 6 seconds. (See Known Gaps: a written guide described 8 seconds and 2 charges; the live tooltip showed 6 seconds and no cooldown field. Treat as a short, repeatable damage-reduction button.)
  - *RaidLens usage:* This is the bread-and-butter active mitigation. For any predictable lethal hit or stacking raid damage window, it should be active. If a player died to a telegraphed mechanic and Survival of the Fittest was not active in the seconds before death, flag it as a missed defensive.
- **Aspect of the Turtle** (spell ID 186265) — 8-second near-immunity: causes attacks to miss, reduces damage taken by 30%, and grants immunity to interrupts/silences, but the Hunter cannot attack while active. Cooldown 3 minutes.
  - *RaidLens usage:* The emergency "oh no" button. Because it stops the Hunter from attacking, occasional use is fine and expected on heavy hits. Used reactively to survive a one-shot mechanic = correct. Never used across an entire fight where players were dying to avoidable damage = possible flag, but weigh against its 3-minute cooldown.
- **Roar of Sacrifice** (spell ID 53480) — external: reduces a friendly target's damage taken by 15% for 10 seconds, 2-minute cooldown.
  - *RaidLens usage:* This is a raid-support cooldown cast ON another player (often a tank or a soaker). Its absence is a coordination/assignment miss rather than a self-survival mistake. Useful to note if the raid had a known damage window with an assigned external and the Hunter never cast it.

## Utility

- **Interrupt — Counter Shot** (spell ID 147362): 40 yd range, **24-second cooldown**, locks the interrupted spell school for several seconds. This IS a raid-usable ranged interrupt and is the spec's interrupt. Marksmanship has a real kick — if a log shows a missed/un-interrupted cast that this Hunter was in range and off-cooldown for, that is a legitimate missed-interrupt flag.
  - Note: Counter Shot is a talent node; if a Hunter did not talent it, they would have NO interrupt. In a raid context assume it is taken, but a complete absence of Counter Shot casts across a fight with interruptible casts may mean it was untalented — worth flagging as "no interrupt casts recorded."
- **Crowd control:** **Intimidation** (pet stun), **Binding Shot** (ground AoE stun on movement), **Freezing Trap** (incapacitate). These are situational raid CC, not interrupts.
- **Dispel — Tranquilizing Shot** (spell ID 343246): removes Enrage and Magic effects from an enemy (an offensive dispel of enemy buffs). It does NOT dispel friendly players and is not a defensive dispel. (See Known Gaps: the live spell tooltip did not cleanly expose the dispel-type field; effect described per the Icy Veins spell summary.)
- **Threat tool — Misdirection** (spell ID 34477): transfers the Hunter's threat to a target for a short window, 30-second cooldown. Threat management, not a defensive or interrupt.
- **Raid buff/debuff:** Hunter's Mark (single-target damage-taken increase). See Role in Raid.
- **Movement tools:** Disengage (spell ID 781, backward leap), Aspect of the Cheetah (spell ID 186257, +90% then sustained speed, 3-minute cooldown), instant shots castable while moving.
