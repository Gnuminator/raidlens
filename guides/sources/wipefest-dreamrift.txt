
Strategy Guide
FIGHT OVERVIEW
Single phase fight with frequent add spawns.

Averzian is trying to claim 3 adjacent spaces on the ground (tic-tac-toe). Don't let him get 3 in a row.

Abyssal Voidshapers claim the spaces for him. There are 3 each set. Put the 2 soaks on the 2 Abyssal Voidshapers you want to kill to remove their 99% damage reduction.

Kill and interrupt any adds.

Phase 1
Averzian is trying to claim 3 adjacent spaces on the ground (tic-tac-toe) throughout the fight.



Prevent him from doing so or you will wipe.



This fight has several additional adds that spawn after a space is claimed.



They just spawn around with a "set" of mechanics. Interrupt their cast and AoE them down.

Strategy Guide
FIGHT OVERVIEW
Single phase and fast-paced fight.

Crystal Walls will trap you in the middle of the room.

Adds fixate on players. They explode when they die. Use the explosion to break the Crystal Walls.

Break the walls to create space to move or the beam will kill you.

Phase 1
Vorasius will trap you with Crystal Walls.



Dodge the AoEs and kite the fixating adds.



Use them to break the walls in order to be able to dodge the beam.

Everyone

Don't get pulled off the platform.

SHARE LINK

Primordial Roar
Pull and knockback
The boss channels, pulling players toward it, and deals some raid-wide damage.



At the end of the channel, Vorasius deals more raid-wide damage and knocks players back.

WHAT TO DO
Stay on the platform
Everyone

Tanks soak and swap after two smashes

Dodge the expanding ring!

SHARE LINK

Shadowclaw Slam
Tank soak
A circle is marked on the floor. The tank needs to move inside to soak this or the raid will wipe. Soaking the first two circles applies a stackable 150% physical vulnerability debuff.



The first two Slams spawn Void Crystal walls across the left and right sides of the room. Don't get stuck on the wrong side!



You'll destroy these Void Crystal Walls with add explosions.



After a few seconds, the circle explodes, creating Aftershocks.



A ring is marked around the circle soak and will explode, dealing damage to anyone inside. The ring continues to expand outwards and explodes multiple times.

WHAT TO DO
Soak and dodge
Everyone

Strategy Guide
FIGHT OVERVIEW
Single phase encounter with an intermission.

Two boss encounter. Both dragons can be attacked during the phase. Neither can be attacked during the intermission.

Break tethers when you are tethered.

Soak the Gloom orb as it travels across the room to reduce its area of denial and damage.

Lock down the Voidorbs with crowd control.

Stay safe inside the barrier during the intermission.

Phase 1
Tank the dragons apart and keep their health between 10% of each others to avoid the damaging buff.



Tank should use the beam aimed at them (not at a random marked player) to reduce the tether effect.



Interrupt and CC the adds, soak the Gloom orb.

Everyone

Run away to break your tether

https://www.mythictrap.com/vs-dr-mqd/vaelgor-and-ezzorak/normal?ability=vaeezzNullz
SHARE LINK

Nullzone
Run away
Tethers all players to Vaelgor and pulls them toward him (the pull is strong!). Tethered players take increasing damage over time.



Each time a player breaks their tether, the raid takes some damage.

WHAT TO DO
Break your tether
Everyone

Dip into the beam to reduce Nullzone pull.

https://www.mythictrap.com/vs-dr-mqd/vaelgor-and-ezzorak/normal?ability=vaeezzNullb
SHARE LINK

Nullbeam
Targeted beam
Large cone-shaped beam toward the tank that lasts 4s. Standing in beam deals damage and applies stacks of a debuff. Each stack weakens strength of the Nullzone pull.



If your class is slow, you can dip quickly into this beam to get out of Nullzone tethers more easily.

WHAT TO DO
Dip quickly into beam
Everyone

Avoid being hit and dispel feared players.

https://www.mythictrap.com/vs-dr-mqd/vaelgor-and-ezzorak/normal?ability=vaeezzDreBre
SHARE LINK

Dread Breath
Fear

Feared players will run away uncontrollably. The fear can be dispelled.

WHAT TO DO
Avoid being hit
Everyone

Touch the orb to soak it as it passes.

Gloom

Strategy Guide
FIGHT OVERVIEW
3 Phase encounter with an Intermission after P1

L'ura has a unique Memory game mechanic. She'll flash Runes in a pattern and mark players with them, and you'll have to match the pattern.

Tons of glaives will fill the room and bounce around. Lots of dodging!

Healed Crystals will turn to Dawn Crystals. Pick them up and keep them safe, you'll use them in P3 to protect the group from deadly blasts.

Use beams to destroy Void Cores in Phase 2.

In Phase 3, stay in the safety of the light from Crystal holders.

Heroic Changes
Dawn Crystals and holders should avoid Cosmic damage. Crystals being hit by Cosmic mechanics will create soak circles.

Beams in Phase 2 now need to be soaked together. Sort out groups and a plan ahead of time.

Phase 3 has an additional mechanic. Soak the circles to drain them before they explode.
